About Our Platform

About EventOps AI

Intelligent event management powered by Agentic AI, designed to simplify planning, automate operations, and deliver exceptional event experiences.

Our Project

EventOps AI is an Agent-powered event management platform developed to streamline the planning, execution, and monitoring of large-scale events such as conferences, hackathons, exhibitions, and corporate summits.

The platform brings event operations together in one place, helping organisers manage registrations, venues, speakers, sponsorships, and incidents while providing analytics and intelligent recommendations.

Our Mission

To make event management smarter and more efficient through automation, collaborative AI agents, real-time insights, and better decision-making.

What Makes Us Different?

Specialised AI agents working together as one platform.

Smart Registration

Simplifies attendee registration, ticket management, check-ins, and attendance tracking.

Venue Optimisation

Helps monitor room capacity, venue utilisation, seating arrangements, and resource availability.

Speaker Coordination

Supports speaker scheduling, availability tracking, session planning, and conflict detection.

Sponsorship Tracking

Organises sponsor commitments, deliverables, visibility metrics, and return-on-investment tracking.

Incident Management

Helps record operational issues, prioritise incidents, coordinate responses, and support escalation workflows.

Event Intelligence

Combines operational information to calculate event health scores, identify potential bottlenecks, and recommend improvements.
